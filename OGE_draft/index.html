Oil and Gas Agent Operating Map

A more concrete segment draft for oil and gas production, refining, field operations, maintenance, process safety, permitting, and governed enterprise execution. The goal is to turn agent ideas into discovery-ready operating patterns: who uses it, what triggers it, what systems it touches, what it outputs, and where a human must approve.

Segment lens

Production and field operationsWell, facility, field crew, production variance, and exception workflows.
Refining and plant operationsOperator rounds, process safety, maintenance windows, and shift handoff.
Maintenance and inspectionAsset health, work orders, inspections, turnaround readiness, and closeout evidence.
Permit, MOC, and compliancePermit-to-work, Management of Change, environmental reporting, and audit trails.

Oil and gas agent cards

Each card is written as an operating pattern, not a product category. Use the filters to focus on the part of the O&G operating model that matters for the customer conversation.

Distinct O&G maturity sequences

These sequences avoid repeating the same Grid/Safety pattern and instead show different paths from Work IQ through governed action.

Visible governance bands

These should be surfaced as badges, discovery prompts, or implementation gates on Oil and Gas cards so autonomy does not sound unconstrained.

Operator-in-the-loopSafety-critical recommendations remain advisory until an authorized operator, engineer, or supervisor approves.
Permit and MOC boundaryChanges to equipment, procedures, or work conditions route through permit-to-work and Management of Change controls.
Evidence trailInputs, citations, risk rationale, approvals, write-backs, and closeout artifacts are retained for audit and learning.
Cyber and identity controlOT boundaries, least privilege, role-based actions, session logs, and governed system write-back define what the agent can do.
